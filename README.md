# BillDesk

Think before you print.

A staff-ID login for advisors, cashiers and super admins. Advisors upload digital PDFs; service amounts, dates and customer identifiers are extracted automatically. Cashiers collect related vehicle bills together, record insurance shares or approved credit, and issue QR gate passes. Admins inspect customer-grouped bills, full collections, unpaid invoices, original PDFs and an audited history.

For Vercel setup, environment variables and database prerequisites, see [DEPLOYMENT.md](DEPLOYMENT.md).

## Local demo

Run npm install, then npm run dev. In .env.local, set SERVICE_DESK_DEMO=true with no Supabase credentials. Demo mode is development-only and cannot run in a production build.

| User ID | Password | Workspace |
| --- | --- | --- |
| cashier01 | CashierDemo!2026 | Mark bills paid |
| advisor01 | AdvisorDemo!2026 | PDF uploads and own bills |
| admin01 | AdminDemo!2026 | Reports, bill drill-down, staff accounts |

These are also the owner-requested initial credentials for the shared database after bootstrap. In local demo mode, staff changes, approvals and collections persist in this browser (localStorage); uploaded PDFs use IndexedDB. Separate demo devices do not share workflow changes. The shared database uses salted password hashes and server sessions instead.

The local workspace automatically parses and imports these owner-supplied PDFs from the project directory:

| File | Owner-provided type | Extracted invoice | Extracted total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ATA545.pdf | Counter sales | AA26-01545 | ₹14,881 |
| ACA1145.pdf | Accessories · non-TGA | ACA/26-27/1145 | ₹40,752 |
| INA2600101.pdf | Insurance claim | INA26-00101 | ₹41,305 |

The displayed fields are generated by the same parser as uploaded PDFs, not copied from this table. The type labels are owner-provided annotations. The records are submitted for review, with no fabricated approvals or collections. The cashier starts with today’s submissions; search finds historical bills. Original PDFs are accessible through View original PDF.

The six old built-in dummy bills and their related simulated history are removed on refresh. A pre-import browser snapshot is saved under service-desk-before-reference-import; other uploads, staff and approvals are preserved. Content hashes and page ranges make repeat imports idempotent. The source PDFs remain on disk and are excluded from version control; they are never copied to public/. This local import and PDF-serving endpoint are disabled in production. Synthetic financial records are used only in unit tests, not the application.

Extracted invoice totals cannot be manually changed. Collection amounts and approved settlement arrangements are recorded separately. The parser reads labelled service invoice totals, invoice dates and customer identifiers from digital PDFs. Repeated headers on continuation pages stay in one invoice. Missing/ambiguous totals require a corrected digital PDF; scanned-image OCR is not included.

Advisors can select up to ten PDFs and click **Upload & read all**. Up to four files upload and process concurrently in the connected workspace. A percentage bar counts PDFs ready for review, while each file shows its upload percentage or reading status. Failed files remain available for retry and do not count as ready. Review cards preserve selection order and show the filename and page range; **View bill** opens the original PDF at that bill's starting page. **Submit for cashier review** confirms each PDF separately, retaining failed submissions for retry. Every remaining bill from a multi-bill PDF is submitted together. Before submitting, advisors can enter an optional token number for each bill from the separate token machine. Leading zeros and prefixes are preserved. The token appears in the cashier list and bill details and can be searched.

Advisors can delete their own unsubmitted drafts from the review card or bill dialog after confirmation. Submitted/approved bills cannot be deleted. A server-side upload claim prevents deletion racing with submission or another deletion. Other bills sharing the PDF remain; deleting the final bill also removes the upload and requests deletion of its stored PDF. Storage cleanup failures are reported explicitly. No new database migration is required for these actions.

Uploaded documents are classified from PDF headings, invoice prefixes, billed parties, job references and repair descriptions. Filenames are not classification inputs. Supported samples include customer service, body-and-paint customer/insurance invoices, Toyota/Assurant extended warranty, warranty repair notes, free maintenance (FMSC) and Toyota Smiles/AMC service notes. Generic warranty terms printed on an ordinary invoice do not make it a warranty bill. Unknown formats remain unclassified or generic tax invoices.

The vehicle owner/consignee is stored as the customer; the billed party is retained separately, so a warranty provider or insurer does not replace the customer name. Classification and its evidence are persisted in `bills.field_confidence.document` alongside the existing extraction metadata. The original customer ID, VIN, registration and job reference remain available for verification. Warranty/AMC notes with no numeric total show “No amount stated”; they remain in the archive and related customer documents, without fabricated zero-value invoices, pending collection requests or payments. Numeric invoices stay in the cashier Overview until marked paid.

The twelve historical PDFs in `bills/` have content-based regression checks in `lib/pdf/extract.test.ts`. Their expected customer values are kept in the ignored local file `samples/private/bill-expectations.json`; these private checks skip when the fixture or PDFs are absent. The reviewed import contains eight monetary invoices and four supporting notes, grouped into ten customers; `output/bill-review/verified-bills.csv` records the verified classifications and linkage. These are shared records uploaded through the advisor workflow, not automatic demo fixtures.

The cashier Overview defaults to bills submitted today in Asia/Kolkata. The date picker filters both the pending list and daily submission summary. Searching spans all submitted history, including paid bills and receivables; clearing search restores the selected day. Submission date is shown beside invoice date, so reused daily token numbers remain distinguishable. Tokens are optional and are never used as bill identity. Numeric token sorting remains in place, with missing tokens last.

Opening a bill selects outstanding customer balances for the same identified customer and normalized vehicle across all dates. Cashiers can deselect invoices. One confirmation records an atomic collection with a shared reference and per-invoice allocations. Payment methods remain Cash, GPay, Cheque, Demand Draft and Other; GPay/DD require a transaction ID, Cheque a cheque number, and Other a description. Initial part payment uses the credit or insurance workflow. Subsequent receivable collections may be partial. Invoice totals remain PDF-derived; recording a payment does not charge a payment gateway.

**Insurance** records a variable customer share per invoice and the insurer name. Once the customer share is paid, release is permitted while the insurer balance remains outstanding. Insurer receipts are recorded separately. **Credit** records zero or part payment, outside approver, approval date, due date, contact details and responsible staff. The cashier records the external approval; there is no new manager login or in-app approval queue. The pink Credit Form – Service shows invoice amounts, received amounts, balances, authorized credit, two follow-ups and remarks, and prints in landscape. An admin can revoke an incorrect approval with a reason; a corrected arrangement retains the prior case and audit history.

**Receivables** contains outstanding insurance and credit balances across all dates, including revoked arrangements requiring review. **Archive** contains fully paid bills and supporting notes. Daily reporting uses submission date and displays current actual collections and outstanding balances; a credit release is never reported as payment. Reversals retain the original payer and collection reference. Payments and approval changes are audited.

Customer groups use customers.id as the primary key and bills.customer_id as the foreign key: one customer can have many bills. An exact normalized Customer ID from the PDF is the preferred identity, including across multiple vehicles. If absent, exact normalized name + VIN (or registration) forms a provisional group. Names alone never merge records; bills without sufficient identity remain separate. Documents with and without Customer ID are not silently merged across those identity types. Invoice/job-card references identify bills or visits, not customers. The group totals and related-bill links respect the logged-in role's visibility.

## WhatsApp bill sharing

The upload review groups bills by customer across PDFs. Enter one WhatsApp number per customer; tokens and invoice details remain per bill. Ten-digit Indian mobile numbers default to +91; other countries require an explicit international prefix. **Submit & open WhatsApp** submits a single customer’s batch and opens one message containing a numbered heading and private link for every bill in that batch, including no-amount warranty/AMC notes. Older bills are excluded. The advisor presses Send. For multiple customers, **Submit all** creates a separate **Open WhatsApp** action for each customer. A blocked popup leaves a visible link.

**Submit all without WhatsApp**, or the customer-group skip checkbox, explicitly bypasses sharing. A failed PDF keeps its customer’s message blocked until retry succeeds or the draft is removed; successful submissions remain saved. The tab remembers only the pending batch’s bill IDs in session storage so a reload can recover its already-submitted bills. A WhatsApp preparation failure never reverses submission: retry sharing without uploading again. **My bills** retains historical sharing of all submitted bills for the selected customer owned by that advisor, including earlier uploads. BillDesk records preparation and explicit skips; it cannot verify that a message was sent, delivered, or read.

Customer downloads use short BillDesk links (`/b/<22-character-code>`) valid for seven days, serving private PDFs containing only each bill’s page range. BillDesk streams each PDF without exposing the storage address; customers do not need a staff login. Only SHA-256 hashes of the random link codes are stored in the server-only `customer_bill_links` table. All links must be prepared successfully before a message is returned; a failed link does not produce an incomplete message. Combined originals remain staff-only. Anyone holding a customer link can open it until expiry; correcting the recipient or preparing another link does not revoke previously issued links. Customer copies are retained with the originals and reused for future sharing. Links expire; retained bills do not. No WhatsApp Business API credentials or messaging service are required.

Set server-only `BILLDESK_PUBLIC_URL=https://billdesk-rho.vercel.app` for production customer links. Local tests explicitly use `http://127.0.0.1:3119`; the host is never inferred from request headers. Previously sent signed storage URLs keep their original expiry.

Token numbers are optional in every submission path. The retired `BILLDESK_REQUIRE_TOKEN` environment setting is ignored. Demo mode supports the same collection and settlement forms; QR issuance still requires the connected database.

1. Configure a development Supabase project. Apply migrations in order:
   - supabase/migrations/202610230001_initial.sql (new projects only)
   - supabase/migrations/202611040001_staff_finance.sql
   - supabase/migrations/202611040002_customer_full_payment.sql
   - supabase/migrations/202611040003_app_auth.sql
   - supabase/migrations/202611050001_atomic_upload_confirmation.sql (apply before deploying the new confirmation endpoint)
   - supabase/migrations/202611050002_cashier_mark_paid.sql (apply before deploying the one-step cashier payment flow)
   - supabase/migrations/202611070001_payment_method_and_token.sql (apply before deploying payment methods and token entry)
   - supabase/migrations/202611100001_admin_chosen_password.sql (apply before deploying admin-chosen staff passwords)
   - supabase/migrations/202611100002_transaction_id_and_delete_staff.sql (apply before deploying transaction IDs and permanent staff deletion)
   - supabase/migrations/202611100003_required_payment_references.sql (requires references for new GPay, Demand Draft and Cheque collections)
   - supabase/migrations/202611110001_whatsapp_sharing.sql (apply immediately before deploying WhatsApp sharing; replaces the confirmation RPC signature)
   - supabase/migrations/202611140001_customer_bill_links.sql (additive server-only mappings for short customer links)
   - supabase/migrations/202611140002_gate_passes.sql (security staff and QR gate passes)
   - supabase/migrations/202611140003_bill_attachments.sql (attachment auditing; compatible with older link preparation)
   - supabase/migrations/202611200001_settlements.sql (batch collections, insurance/credit arrangements, optional tokens)
   - supabase/migrations/202611200002_gate_pass_documents.sql (numbered printable QR gate-pass documents)
2. Set NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL and SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY in .env.local. Set SERVICE_DESK_DEMO=false. The publishable/anon key is no longer required. Never expose the secret/service-role key in frontend variables. Supabase supplies database and private PDF storage; staff login does not use Supabase Auth, emails or Auth dashboard settings.
3. Run npm run bootstrap:staff. It installs individually salted scrypt hashes for admin01 / AdminDemo!2026, cashier01 / CashierDemo!2026 and advisor01 / AdvisorDemo!2026. Existing profile IDs and bill history are preserved. A repeat run does not overwrite passwords already configured in staff_credentials. The three initial accounts are usable immediately; additional admin-created accounts use the 6–8 character password (any characters; no complexity requirements) chosen by the admin and can sign in immediately. Password resets still issue temporary passwords that must be changed at first login. Known initial passwords are not made private by hashing; reset them before opening the app to untrusted users.
4. Restart the app. The admin can create further individual staff accounts, reset passwords, disable access or permanently delete accounts. Deletion removes the profile, credentials and sessions; existing bills and collection history remain, with removed staff shown as “Deleted staff account”. The signed-in account and admin01 cannot be deleted. User IDs use 3–40 lowercase letters, digits or underscores. There is no public signup or email recovery; the admin resets staff access.
5. Before enabling the new app, stop any existing external purge schedules. The migration clears expiry dates, enforces permanent retention and unschedules matching Supabase cron jobs. Deploy the retired purge-paid function too if the original function was previously deployed. It now returns 410 and deletes nothing.

Paid bills, PDFs and audit records are retained. Existing bills without financial values remain pending and excluded from financial totals. Admin can open those bills and choose “Re-read original PDF”; the cashier can then mark the bill paid once payment is collected. No historical collection is inferred from the old paid marker. PDFs already deleted by the previous retention policy cannot be recovered by this migration.

Production demo fallback is disabled: a production build without Supabase configuration shows a setup notice instead of simulated authentication.

The app-auth migration detaches profiles from auth.users without deleting existing Auth users or business records. Old Supabase Auth JWTs lose direct access to the app's tables, RPCs and PDF storage. Existing staff beyond the three initial accounts need an administrator password reset to initialize their new credential record. Previously generated Supabase Auth passwords no longer sign in to BillDesk.

Passwords are verified on the server with Node's scrypt (N=32768, r=8, p=3), a random salt and constant-time comparison. Only the encoded hash reaches staff_credentials; no plaintext password is stored in Supabase or audit events. Use HTTPS when deployed: the browser submits the password to the app server, which hashes it before database writes. Database hashes are not accepted as login passwords.

Sessions use random HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict cookies (Secure in production), expire after 12 hours and store only SHA-256 token digests in staff_sessions. Logout, password changes/resets and account access changes revoke sessions. Sign-in is limited to 10 attempts per staff ID per 15 minutes. POST routes reject cross-origin requests. Credential/session tables and identity-bearing RPCs are server-only; every app route validates the session and role, and database functions enforce financial and staff-management permissions too.

## Reporting rules

- Admin Overview shows three clickable counts across all dates, optionally filtered by uploading advisor.
- Total paid bills: submitted invoices currently paid in full, with their total invoice amount underneath. Each bill counts once; reversals move it back to unpaid.
- Total unpaid bills: all submitted invoices not currently paid in full, including those awaiting approval, with their remaining balance underneath.
- Awaiting approval: the subset of unpaid invoices not yet approved by the cashier. Drafts and supporting warranty/AMC notes are excluded from all three counts. Unread amounts are counted as unpaid bills but excluded from monetary totals and flagged.
- INR is stored in integer paise. Dates are displayed and grouped in Asia/Kolkata.
- Every clickable metric uses the same snapshot and filters as its underlying rows. Search filters the displayed rows only and is labelled accordingly.
- Reversing an incorrect payment requires a super admin and a reason. The original payment remains. The negative correction belongs to the reversal date. This is not a refund or credit note.
- Credit and insurance arrangements support partial collections. Database checks enforce nonnegative customer/insurer balances and exact reversal amounts. Release clearance is separate from payment status.
- On upgrade, old payment fragments are preserved in archived records. A previously fully settled set is represented by one full-bill collection. Incomplete legacy collections remain archived and require cashier reconciliation; nothing is silently topped up. The transition is audited. Existing demo data is upgraded without deleting uploads or staff accounts.
- Bill totals are not profit or tax-exclusive accounting revenue. Parts/labour analysis, refunds, invoice cancellation, OCR and external platform synchronisation are not included.

## Interfaces and future integration

Internal authenticated routes:
- POST /api/auth/login: staffId and password; sets an HttpOnly app session cookie and returns the profile, never a token or password hash.
- POST /api/auth/logout: revokes the current app session and clears its cookie.
- GET /api/desk: current profile and a consistent bill/payment/staff/audit snapshot.
- POST /api/desk: password, approve, collect, collect_batch, arrange, update_settlement, revoke_settlement, reverse, pdf, reextract, create_staff, reset_staff, toggle_staff or delete_staff. Settlement actions require a request UUID; collection allocations use integer paise and a customer/insurer payer.
- Existing upload init/process endpoints remain; only advisors can use them.
- POST /api/uploads/[id]/confirm: `tokens` keyed by bill ID. Advisors submit bills to the cashier without customer phone numbers or a WhatsApp choice. The server derives the existing transaction's no-sharing contacts for the owned bills and ignores legacy client contact payloads. Token format validation remains server-enforced, tokens are optional, and confirmation remains atomic per PDF and retry-safe.
- POST /api/bills/[id]/whatsapp and GET /api/bills/[id]/attachment: retired; return HTTP 410. Customer bill delivery is handled outside BillDesk. Existing `/b/[code]` links remain valid until their original expiry; staff original-PDF viewing is unchanged.
- POST /api/demo/extract: real PDF parsing for development-only demo uploads; disabled in production.
- GET /api/demo/reference-bills: parse the three fixed local reference PDFs. The file query parameter serves only one of those three originals. Disabled in production.

Financial mutations execute through checked database functions, row locking and an append-only payment ledger. Direct browser database access is disabled; the server returns role-scoped snapshots and authorizes short-lived signed PDF URLs. Account status and temporary-password requirements are checked for backend actions.

Submission uses `app_confirm_upload` to confirm the upload and insert its submitted audit events in one transaction. A partial unique index on confirmed PDF hashes prevents duplicate submissions across staff, including simultaneous requests; a retry of the same upload returns its original confirmation without another audit event. The stored SHA-256 hash is reused, so submission does not download or parse the PDF again. Distinct PDFs sharing a job/customer remain valid; changed PDF bytes with the same invoice reference are still protected separately at invoice approval. Apply the confirmation migration before deploying the endpoint. Existing duplicate confirmed hashes cause the migration to stop without deleting or merging documents.

lib/desk.ts contains reporting calculations and typed records. Stable bill/payment/staff IDs and timestamps are retained for a future versioned read API. External API credentials, pagination, company/centre mapping and platform-specific synchronisation belong to that later integration phase.

## Gate passes after payment

Cashiers can **Issue gate pass** from the paid bill dialog or Archive. The server checks every submitted bill for the identified customer and normalized vehicle, across advisors, and lists blockers. Supporting notes without payable amounts do not block clearance. The cashier enters the customer WhatsApp number in the gate-pass section. The field starts blank and never falls back to a bill/advisor phone number; only the cashier’s entry is used. The cashier presses Send; the WhatsApp icon remains available if the popup was blocked.

Customers open `/g/[code]` without login to see their QR, registration, name, pass number, expiry and status. Passes last 24 hours and permit one exit. Reissuing an active pass preserves its code and expiry; replacing an expired or revoked unused pass repeats payment checks. A completed pass cannot cover a later visit, which requires newly submitted bills. New bills require replacement coverage even after settlement; reversing a covered payment revokes unused passes.

Administrators create **Security** staff through the existing account controls. These users enter `/security`, scan with the rear camera or paste a BillDesk gate-pass link, compare the vehicle and tap **Confirm exit**. Verification requires a working connection. Security accounts have no access to financial records, invoice PDFs, uploads or staff management. See [deployment instructions](DEPLOYMENT.md#qr-gate-passes) for the migration, signing secret, validation and two-phone pilot.

## Printable vehicle gate passes

Gate passes use the Nippon Toyota service form: unique digital serial, issue date/time in IST, customer, registration, R.O./W.O. references/dates, tax invoice references/dates, issuing cashier and a signature line. Missing job details can be entered before issuance; missing values are printed as “Not provided.” The linked invoices are snapshotted on issuance. Reissuing an active pass preserves its QR, expiry and saved document; it does not silently rewrite a document already shared.

The customer’s WhatsApp link opens a mobile layout with a Print gate pass button and an A5 portrait print layout. The QR uses the existing private verification link. Security still verifies online and confirms one exit. Expiry, payment reversal, revoked credit approval and newly submitted bills are checked by the server. No financial or credit-contact details are included in the public pass.

## Settlement verification

Run `npm test`, `npm run lint`, and `npm run build`. `npm run test:database` runs migrations and synthetic transactional checks in PGlite (set `PGLITE_PATH` to an installed test-only package if needed). `scripts/check-settlements-browser.mjs` exercises a local demo server, with `PLAYWRIGHT_PATH` and `DESK_URL` overrides. Generated screenshots and printable samples are kept under ignored `output/settlement-checks/`.

Customer bill selection: apply `supabase/migrations/202611280001_customer_bill_selection.sql` after the settlements migrations before deploying. Cashiers can select outstanding bills for one customer, including different vehicles, and collect their combined remaining balance in one payment. Credit and insurance arrangements remain per vehicle.
